The organization

The New Foreign Assistance is dedicated to promoting effective, accountable, and finite U.S. foreign assistance that advances American interests and human welfare through economic growth, trade, and investment.

A “do tank” based in Washington, D.C., we exist to move policy and programming.

The New Foreign Assistance does not seek or accept federal funding.
